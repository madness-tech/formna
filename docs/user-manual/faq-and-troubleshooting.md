## Common questions

### Can I save a form and finish later?
Yes. Draft saving is supported for normal in-progress work.

### Can I edit a submission after submitting it?
Only if the form settings allow post-submission editing.

### What is the difference between a form and a program?
A form is one submission unit. A program combines multiple forms into one larger application process.

### What does “clarification requested” mean?
It means an administrator needs more information or a correction before continuing review.

## Login issues

If you cannot log in:

- verify your email and password
- ensure your email has been verified
- reset your password if needed
- check whether MFA is required
- contact your administrator if your account may be inactive

---

## Form and upload issues

If a form will not submit:

- review all required fields
- look for validation messages
- confirm uploads finished successfully
- refresh and try again if your connection was unstable

If a file upload fails:

- verify file size
- verify file type
- try another browser if the problem persists

---

## Status and review questions

Review timelines vary by organization. In general:

- **submitted** means your form or application was received
- **in review** means staff are evaluating it
- **approved** and **rejected** are final decisions unless your organization documents an appeal process

---

## Getting help

When asking for help, include:

- your name and account email
- the action you were trying to complete
- the exact problem or error message
- your browser and device
- screenshots when useful
