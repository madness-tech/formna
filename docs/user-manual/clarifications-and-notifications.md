## Clarification requests

Administrators may request clarification when they need more detail, a correction, or a better document.

This does **not** automatically mean your submission has been rejected.

Common clarification types include:

- information request
- rectification request

---

## How to respond

When you receive a clarification:

1. open the request from the notification or clarification area
2. read the message carefully
3. answer each requested item clearly
4. upload corrected or additional files if requested
5. submit your response

Good responses are prompt, complete, and directly tied to the administrator’s question.

---

## Notification types

You may receive in-app and email notifications for events such as:

- successful form submissions
- successful program submissions
- clarification requests
- clarification updates
- approvals or rejections
- deadline reminders
- draft reminders

---

## Managing notifications

You can usually:

- open the notification bell to review recent activity
- mark notifications as read
- open the full notification list if available
- manage email preferences in account settings

If email messages are missing, first check spam or junk folders.
