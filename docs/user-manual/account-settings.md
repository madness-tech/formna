## Profile updates

In account settings, you can typically update:

- first name
- last name
- profile details supported by your organization

---

## Password and security

Common actions include:

- changing your password
- enabling MFA
- changing your MFA device
- disabling MFA when organizational policy allows it

If you forget your password, use the password reset flow from the login page.

---

## Email, language, and timezone

You may be able to manage:

- your email address, often with verification steps
- your preferred interface language
- your timezone for accurate date and deadline display

---

## Notification preferences

Where enabled, you can control email-notification preferences for important events such as submissions, clarifications, and reminders.

---

## Account support scenarios

Contact an administrator if you need help with:

- account activation issues
- MFA reset
- email change problems
- organization-specific policy questions
