## Create your account

To register:

1. open the registration page
2. enter your first name, last name, email address, and password
3. confirm any required profile fields such as timezone
4. submit the registration form

Choose a strong password that meets your organization’s requirements.

---

## Verify your email

After registration:

1. check your inbox
2. open the verification email
3. click the verification link
4. return to the login page if prompted

If the email does not arrive, check spam or use the resend option.

---

## Log in

To sign in:

1. enter your email address and password
2. complete any additional security step such as MFA
3. continue to your dashboard

---

## Set up MFA if required

Some organizations require or encourage MFA.

Typical setup:

1. install an authenticator app
2. scan the QR code shown by the platform
3. enter the current six-digit code
4. save your recovery codes securely

---

## Understand your dashboard

Your dashboard usually shows:

- available forms
- available programs
- drafts and completed submissions
- pending clarification requests
- recent notifications

Use it as your main starting point each time you log in.
